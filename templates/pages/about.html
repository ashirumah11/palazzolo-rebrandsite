Our History & Mission

Palazzolo Technical and Vocational Training Institute (PTVTI) was established in November 2021 by the Poverelle Sisters in the aftermath of the COVID-19 pandemic. Witnessing the severe disruptions experienced by young people and families in Kiambu and Nairobi metropolitan areas, the Sisters recognized the urgent need for accessible, practical technical education to restore hope and build self-reliance.

What began as an emergency mission to empower vulnerable youth has grown into an accredited center of vocational excellence. Today, the institute celebrates more than 150 graduates employed across technical industries or managing their own entrepreneurial enterprises.
